Interactive Case Study: Bellabeat Smart Device Trends

An in-depth presentation of Google Analytics apprenticeship results, complete with live data simulations.

Core Objectives & Stakeholders

Defining the Business Challenge

Bellabeat is a high-tech company that manufactures health-focused smart products for women. By analyzing smart device fitness data, we aimed to uncover how consumers utilize non-Bellabeat smart devices to guide strategic marketing pathways for their product line.

  • Primary Goal: Understand weekly activity and sleep habits of active users.
  • Strategic Aim: Map non-intrusive opportunities to prompt behavioral changes.
  • Stakeholders: Urška Sršen (Co-founder & Chief Creative Officer), Marketing Analytics team.
Key Question Raised

"How do consumer habits on generic smart devices translate to potential feature updates or campaigns for Bellabeat products?"

Data Gathering & Security

Data Ingestion and Integrity Check

We utilized public smart device tracker datasets (FitBit Fitness Tracker Data), consisting of minute-level physical activity, heart rate, and sleep monitoring records from 30+ consenting users.

  • Data Range: Cohorts monitored across daily and hourly cycles.
  • Validation Parameters: Checked row uniqueness, timestamp consistency, and NULL tracking indices.
  • Limitations Identified: High levels of missing manual parameters (specifically BMI weight metrics).

Robust Python Data Cleaning Pipeline

Using Pandas inside Google Colab, raw activity and sleep datasets were merged. Dates were transformed into normalized datetime elements, column headers standardized, and outliers pruned.

  • Converted non-standard date strings to Pandas datetime64 format.
  • Unified inconsistent ID representations across sleep and daily summaries.
  • Built strict logging scripts tracing row drop-offs in Google Sheets and Python environment.

Strategic Direction & Implementation

Tactical Business Recommendations

To maximize user engagement, Bellabeat should shift marketing and product telemetry features based on these generic device trends.

  • Gentle Daytime Nudges: Prompt localized notifications during long workweek sedentary stretches to help users break the sedentary state and achieve optimal nightly sleep.
  • Passive Tracking Integrations: Replace manual inputs with connected, automated measurements (smart scales/automatic BMI calculators) to avoid the severe manual entry drop-off.
  • Weekend Challenge Programs: Introduce competitive Saturday step milestones, encouraging community loops when user physical capacity naturally peaks.
Executive Impact Summary

Implementing passive BMI weight integrations holds potential to recover up to 60% of lost tracking engagement.
